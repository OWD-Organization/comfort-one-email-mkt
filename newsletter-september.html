Your AC just survived a full Phoenix summer. Here's its reward.

In the Valley, September still feels like summer some afternoons. But the sprint is over. Back-to-back 115° days. A system that's run nonstop since April. This is the first real stretch of the year where it gets to catch its breath before a short, mild winter shows up.

That makes right now the smartest window all year to get it checked. Skip it, and the small stuff that got ignored in July turns into a bigger repair next June. This month: what to do if one room never quite kept up this summer, when we're scheduling fall Comfort Club checkups, and a financing deadline worth knowing about if a replacement's been on your mind.
